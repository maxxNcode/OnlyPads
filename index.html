Questions.

Is this connected to OnlyFans?

No. OnlyPads is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to OnlyFans. It does not access any OnlyFans account and cannot pay money into one. The creator's Cut is paid in SOL to a Solana wallet. OnlyPads borrows the vocabulary of subscriptions — Subs, renewals, Drops — and nothing else.

Where does the money actually go?

60% of every creator fee goes to the creator's wallet, 35% to the OnlyVault for the people holding the coin, and 5% to the Pad. The split is written on chain at launch and cannot be changed afterwards.

What if the creator never claims?

The Cut accrues in Creator Escrow and stays theirs. Nothing is withheld and nothing expires in this build. A fixed abandonment rule — unclaimed Cut returning to the OnlyVault after a set period — is under consideration so funds are not held forever.

Is any of this real?

Yes — and only what is. Coins on the board are real pump.fun launches, verified on chain. Anything we cannot read yet shows a dash, not a guess.

Who is this for?

Adults only (18+). OnlyPads carries no adult content. A coin can only be created for a verified adult creator, and any coin created for or by a minor is taken down and burned. That rule is not negotiable.
