## OnlyPad — concept written

New project. Workspace was empty; created `docs/CONCEPT.md` + this memory dir.

Brief, in one line: *"create a similar idea for claimpad.site called OnlyPad, relating the name itself,
creator rewards go to the community, and launch a coin that routes fees to your favorite creator on
OnlyFans."*

Read `claimpad.site` (CLAIM — Solana/pump.fun launchpad, 80/20 vault/treasury split, Proof of Hold,
15-min epoch pot, dark-mode-GoFundMe UI) and `CLAIM/docs/CONCEPT.md` + `CLAIM/.workbuddy-ai/memory/MEMORY.md`
to inherit the architecture and the design system rather than re-inventing them.

**Resolved the tension in the brief.** The brief asked for two different things at once — "creator
rewards go to the community" (CLAIM's model) *and* "route fees to your favorite creator." Answered
with a **three-way split (60/35/5)**: Cut → creator, Tips → Subs, Pad cut → $ONLY. Explained why a
pure creator payout is a donation widget and a pure holder payout is CLAIM with a new accent.

**Verified rather than assumed the load-bearing claim:** OnlyFans has no official public API — every
reseller is unofficial and carries ban risk. So the Cut is paid in **SOL to a wallet**, and OnlyPad
takes OnlyFans' vocabulary, not its API. Led with this in the reply; it invalidates the literal
reading of the brief.

**Name system** derived from `Only` + `Pad`: the Pad, an Only, OnlyVault, **Sub** (holder),
**Sub Streak**, **Proof of Sub**, The Cut, Tips, PPV, the Tip Jar, a Drop. "Subs" is the strongest
piece — holders are literally called subscribers.

**New versus CLAIM:** third payout rail; **Creator Escrow** on Fan Launch (a fan can gift a creator a
revenue stream — the growth loop); creator verification (signed challenge, VERIFIED/UNCLAIMED badges).

**Decisions made and recorded:** Proof of Sub ladder is in **renewals (30d)**, deliberately slower
than CLAIM's day-based curve. Accent changes from CLAIM's green to **rose `#E8455F`** — avoids
OnlyFans blue, separates the two products. Flagged the **"OnlyPad" trademark risk** and offered
`FanPad` / `CutPad` as fallbacks.

Rendered two inline visuals: the three-way split diagram, and the Proof of Sub curve (piecewise-linear
on a true linear day axis — category labels would have misrepresented it).

Deliverable: `docs/CONCEPT.md`. **Nothing built.** Offered the landing page as the next step.

## OnlyPad — the site is built

Second brief, same day: *"make it a website — copy the UI style of claimpad.site, dark mode, clean
flat modern."* Built the whole landing page as a **port of CLAIM's design system**, not a fresh design.

**How the port was done.** Copied `CLAIM/assets/css/styles.css` and renamed the tokens
(`--green*` → `--accent*`, `--rose` → `--danger`), then appended an OnlyPad-only block. Rationale: the
GoFundMe-dark system is user-specified and CLAIM's own memory says *do not drift*. Re-deriving it
would have been a silent redesign.

**Files:** `index.html`, `assets/css/styles.css`, `assets/js/{math,data,wallet,app,epoch}.js`.
No build step, no dependencies — same conventions as CLAIM. Load order matters: math → data → wallet →
app → epoch.

**Accent is rose `#E8455F`.** CLAIM's green would make the two sites read as one product, and
OnlyFans blue is a trademark. One accent, action-only, as before.

### Bugs found by running it — all three were invisible to reading the code

1. **The displayed split did not sum to 100.** `1 - 0.8` is `0.19999999999999996` in binary floating
   point, so rounding the three fractions independently gave **80/17/2** (99%) and **40/53/8** (101%).
   Fixed by adding `splitPct()` to `math.js`, which does the arithmetic in integer percent space:
   cut = round(cut), pad = round(rest × 0.125), tips = rest − pad. Every cut from 40 to 80 now sums to
   100. **Use `splitPct()` for anything a human reads; `splitOf()` stays for the fractions.**
2. **Only the first stat counter ever animated.** `reveal()` used `querySelector('[data-count]')`
   inside the observer — the stats row holds five counters. Now `querySelectorAll`.
3. **`.board__foot` could sit at `opacity: 0` forever.** A *jump* scroll (a nav anchor, or landing on
   `#faq`) moves an element past the viewport between two frames, so the IntersectionObserver never
   sees it intersect and it is never revealed. Added a rAF-throttled `sweep()` on scroll/resize as a
   safety net. Verified by jumping straight to `#faq` and checking the element: now 37/37 revealed.

Also fixed: `prevBadge` was referenced in `renderSplit()` but **never existed in the markup** (the
preview never showed VERIFIED/UNCLAIMED), and it was being given the `gcard__mode` class, which
absolutely-positions it. And tickers were truncated to 5 chars (`$CASSI`, `$HALCY`) — now the full
handle.

### Copy correctness — the thing worth remembering

The live feed originally said *"The Cut paid to @northstar"* on an **UNCLAIMED** coin. That is false:
on a Fan Launch nothing has been paid, the Cut is in escrow. Now it reads *"Cut accrued in escrow
for @handle"* in amber, with `in escrow` as the sub-label. **The escrow is the product — the copy must
not quietly contradict it.** Same reasoning behind the card's left figure: `Paid to creator` on a
verified coin, `Waiting to claim` in amber on an unclaimed one.

### Verification done (not just "the code reads correctly")

- 46 assertions against `math.js` + `data.js` in Node — curve knots, split summing to 100 across
  40→80, the MIN_HOLD floor, Drop bucketing, dataset invariants.
- Rendered in headless Chrome over **DevTools Protocol** (`file://`, because the sandbox proxies the
  localhost port and the server dies between Bash calls). Verified: 0 console errors, 0 exceptions,
  no horizontal overflow at 1440px or 390px, burger/link swap on mobile, board search + filters,
  the cut slider driving the preview (80 → 80/17/3), FAQ accordion, wallet connect, the floor
  excluding a 60-`$ONLY` wallet, and the Drop skip stacking Drops.
- Screenshots reviewed section by section, not just asserted.

**Everything numeric is simulated and badged `DEMO`.** The 12 creators are invented stage handles.
The wallet connect reports honestly that it generated an address. `docs/CONCEPT.md` scope note updated
to say what is actually built.

## OnlyPad — OnlyFans-inspired component grammar

Fourth brief: *"make the design reference to onlyfans.com make sure the UI components are highly
inspired."*

**The line I drew: the pattern logic, not the brand.** Sourced a teardown of OnlyFans-style creator
apps, and it says the same thing in its own words — *"copy the pattern logic, not the adult-brand
aesthetics"* and *"It should not copy visual clichés blindly."* That is the whole basis for this pass.

**The accent stays rose. Not OnlyFans blue (#00AFF0).** Two reasons, and the first is the real one:
the name already carries trademark risk (same prefix, same commercial space as a registered mark), and
adopting their accent on top of that compounds two identity elements instead of one. The second:
what makes a page read as a *creator platform* is the structure, not the hex. One token flips it to
blue if the user decides otherwise — `--accent` in `:root`.

**What was adopted — the stacked profile hierarchy:**
cover → avatar **overlapping** the cover → identity (name + verified tick + handle) → social proof →
content promise → **an action that never hides** → a locked tile that is *visibly* locked.

**New component set (all `.of*`):** `.ofcover` (+`__badge` top-right, `__mode` top-left, `__mark`
watermark), `.ofav` (overlaps via `margin-top:-32px` + `z-index:2`), `.ofid`, `.oftick`, `.ofpromise`,
`.ofstats`, `.oflock`, `.oflocktile`, `.ofstrip`, `.ofaction`, `.ofcue`, `.ofpanel`.

**The locked tile is the good idea here.** The teardown calls the paywall "the middle ground": a
paywall that hides everything is too blunt, one that reveals too much kills the sale — so show *what*
is locked, *how much*, and *what unlocks it*. That maps exactly onto Creator Escrow: an UNCLAIMED coin
has paid its creator nothing. Dashed amber border, lock glyph, the amount, and "@handle has not
claimed." A locked state has to be visibly distinct, not a tint of the normal one.

**The creator section is now a full profile panel** — cover, unknown-creator avatar, the three stats,
and a **preview strip of locked tiles**, one per unclaimed Only. That is the locked-media-grid idiom
doing real work: it is a list of money sitting there waiting for a creator.

**Stat labels are one word each, in the product's own vocabulary: Subs · Cut · Tips.** First attempt
used "To creator" and "Claimable", which wrapped to a second line and left the row ragged (measured:
rows were uneven). `Subs` hold it, the `Cut` is the creator's share, the `Tips` are the Subs'. Now all
rows are exactly 73px.

**Superseded by the `.of*` set** — these ported CLAIM classes are now unused and can be dropped in a
cleanup pass: `.gcard__banner`, `.gcard__av`, `.gcard__mode`, `.gcard__nums`, `.gcard__esc`, and the
old `.escrow` / `.escrow__amt` / `.escrow__note`. Left in place this turn rather than risk a mass edit.

Verified: 13 cards all rebuilt (13 covers, 13 overlapping avatars, 39 stat cells), 4 locked tiles
matching the 4 unclaimed coins, the card action wired to an honest "not wired up yet" toast, no
overflow at 1440/390, stat rows even, 46 maths assertions still green, 0 console errors.

## OnlyPad — accent switched to the OnlyFans blue

Fifth brief, a clarification: *"What i mean is the color similar to only fans."* So the fourth brief
was about the palette, not the components. Fair — I read it as the component language.

**Done: `--accent: #00AFF0`.** Verified as the brand blue (`#00aff0`, RGB 0/175/240) rather than
guessed. I had flagged the trademark risk twice; they clarified, so it is their call and it is now on
the record in `MEMORY.md` as accepted, with an instruction not to re-litigate or quietly revert it.
The wordmark and logo are still not used — those are what would make it passing off rather than a
similar palette.

**The one real design decision: `--accent-ink` is DARK (`#0E0E0E`), not white.** OnlyFans' own
white-on-blue button is a legibility defect: white on `#00AFF0` measures **2.5:1**, which fails WCAG
AA (4.5:1) and even AA-large (3:1). `#0E0E0E` on the same blue measures **7.7:1** — AAA. Measured
both rather than eyeballing it. A bright accent wants dark text, and on a near-black page the dark ink
also just looks crisper.

All eight `color: #fff` declarations in the stylesheet sat on accent backgrounds (checked by grep
first, not assumed), so they were swapped to `var(--accent-ink)` in one pass.

**Also fixed while in there:** `--blue` (`#4E9CFF`) was doing duty as the PPV mode tag, and with a
blue accent it became indistinguishable from Stream at tag size. Renamed the token to `--violet`
(`#9B7BFF`) so the three modes stay distinct: Stream = accent blue, PPV = violet, Lock = amber.
Also dropped a dead `.tag--milestone` rule left over from the CLAIM port.

**Contrast table, measured in the browser:** button text on accent 7.71 · accent as text on `--bg`
7.71 · on `--surface` 6.95 · on `--surface-2` 6.43. All pass AA. Zero rose left anywhere on the page;
brand mark and favicon are blue with a dark ring to match the ink decision.

## OnlyPad — the live activity feed, and launching for real

Sixth brief: *"follow the live activity where a user launch it will show the launch coin there."*
So: match the reference's hero feed, and make the launch wizard actually produce something.

The reference's hero feed reads **"Live activity — Launches and claims, as they happen"**, and it
shows launches, not only payouts. Mine was titled "Live cuts" and only showed payouts. Fixed.

**The feed now carries three event kinds** — `launch`, `cut`, `tips` — rendered by a new `feedRow()`.
A launch row shows the **split** where a payout row shows an amount, because a coin that has just been
created has not paid anyone yet. Header is now `Live activity / Launches and payouts, as they happen`
with a `live · demo` pill.

**`emitLaunch()` is the new loop.** It builds the Only from the wizard fields, unshifts a feed event,
pushes the coin into the dataset, re-renders feed + board + stats, toasts, and scrolls to the new card.
The toast says plainly that **no real coin was created** — the UI behaves like a launch, the copy never
claims one happened.

**The board sort stays honest.** It is ranked by market cap, and a coin with no trades has none, so a
just-launched Only sorts **last**. Rather than fake a pin, the new coin is made findable three ways:
an `is-fresh` accent ring on the card, a `NEW` badge on the feed row, and a scroll to it. The sort
never lies.

**`tick()` runs every 12s** so "live activity" actually moves: it ages every existing row, unshifts a
simulated payout, refreshes the stat totals, and nudges just that coin's own card — **not a full grid
re-render**, which would churn the DOM and lose hover state every twelve seconds.

### Two real bugs this work exposed

1. **`refreshStats()` must update `dataset.count`, not just the text.** The count-up reads its target
   from `dataset.count`, so updating only the text let a *later* reveal animate the number back down to
   a stale value. That is how "Onlys launched" showed **12 after a thirteenth was launched** — the
   launch set it to 13, then scrolling past the stats row animated it back to 12. Both writers now
   agree.
2. **`reveal()` was leaking an IntersectionObserver per call.** `renderBoard()` calls it on every
   keystroke in the search box, so typing a word built five or six observers. Now a single
   module-level observer is reused.

Also: the locked tile and the card cue now handle a coin with no Cut yet — "Locked — nothing yet /
The Cut accrues from the first trade" and "No Tips yet" — because "Locked — $0.00" reads like a bug.

Verified end to end in the browser: the gate (disabled → wallet → still disabled until the box is
ticked → enabled), a Creator Launch and a Fan Launch, the new coin in the feed with `NEW` + `just now`
and the split, 13 → 14 cards, the `is-fresh` ring, the stat holding at 13 *after* the count-up reveal,
and the ticker producing a new event 12s later with the older labels ageing. 0 console errors.

## OnlyPad — the logo

Seventh brief: a logo based on the pasted OnlyFans mark, reading **"OP"**.

**Deliverables:** `brand/onlypad-mark.svg` (source of truth) and `brand/onlypad-mark-1024.png`.
Wired into the nav, the footer and the favicon — the inline SVG is the same geometry, so there is one
artefact, not three.

**Construction**, measured off the reference rather than eyeballed:
- Full-bleed `#00AFF0` square, no radius (the reference is a sharp square — a rounded app-icon variant
  is one `rx` on the `<rect>`).
- The **o**: circle `cx=34 cy=52 r=20.2` in pale blue `#C7E6F7`, with the counter punched as a
  background-coloured circle `r=7`. Both figures come from measuring the reference at 328px.
- The **P**: white, built from the same skeleton as the reference's "f" so the two letters read as
  siblings — a **pointed hood at the top-right**, a full bowl whose counter starts at the stem's right
  edge (`x=60`) so it reads as a P rather than a leaf, a **chunky tapering stem**, and a tail that
  sweeps down-left to a **sharp point at (47,75) that cuts into the o** the way the f's descender does.
  Single path, `fill-rule="evenodd"` for the counter.

**Iterated visually six times** against the reference side by side in the browser. The failures worth
remembering: v1–v2 were a leaf on a straight stick (bowl too small, stem too thin); v3–v4 had a
needle tail and a visible 90° step where the bowl met the stem, because the counter was inset from the
stem's edge instead of starting at it; v5–v6 thickened the strokes to match the reference's weight.
**Do not edit this path blind — render it next to the reference and look.**

**The trademark risk is now materially higher and this is not a colour question any more.** The mark is
a close sibling of a registered logo, in the same commercial space, under a name that already shares a
prefix with that mark. The blue was arguable; a near-clone of the logo is the strongest form of the
exposure flagged in the risk section. Recorded as the user's accepted decision, but it should be in
front of counsel before launch, and `FanPad` / `CutPad` remain the clean fallback names.

### Then the P was rebuilt — it was not good

Eighth brief: *"the letter P is not good make it clear and clean design."* Correct. v6's P had
inherited the reference **f**'s two most distinctive traits — the pointed hood and the descender that
sweeps to a scythe point — and neither survives the transplant. On an "f" they are letterform-specific;
on a "P" they read as a molten blob.

**The lesson, worth keeping:** the reference's character lives in traits that only work on the letter
they were drawn for. Copying them onto a different letter produces neither a clean letter nor a
faithful homage. Take the *system* — the blue, the pale-blue `o` with a punched counter, the bold
white letter that cuts into it — and then draw the new letter properly.

**The P is now a clean geometric form**, and the strokes are even:
- stem `x 48..58` (10 wide), `y 27..76` — straight, flat foot, no taper, no sweep
- bowl outer `x 58..83`, `y 27..57`, widest at mid-height
- counter `x 58..73`, `y 35..49` — its left edge **is** the stem's right edge, so the bowl reads as a
  closed P and not a leaf
- strokes land at 10 / 10 / 8 / 8 (stem / right / top / bottom) — visually even

Path: `M48 27 L58 27 C72 27 83 33 83 42 C83 51 72 57 58 57 L58 76 L48 76 Z M58 35 C68 35 73 39 73 42
C73 45 68 49 58 49 Z`

Replaced in all three places (nav, footer, favicon — a plain string swap of the `d` attribute), PNG
re-exported. Verified in the nav at 30x30 and at 16px: reads "oP" cleanly at both. 0 console errors.

## OnlyPad — the logo replaced with the user's own design, in blue

Ninth brief: the user pasted their own **green neon "oP"** and asked for it **blue** — *"make it blue
because its green to be like onlyfans logo."* So this is their letterform, recoloured, not mine.

**The single most useful thing I did: zoom the reference to 3x before drawing anything.** I had been
guessing the letterform from a small image across several failed attempts. Cropping and scaling it in
the browser showed the structure immediately, and the key fact I'd missed:

> **The P's stem's left edge is a long straight diagonal** that runs from the bottom point up past the
> o, and it is that line which cuts the o. That diagonal is what gives the mark its lean. My earlier
> versions had a near-vertical stem, which is why they read as flat and the tail read as a needle.

Other details from the zoom: the bowl is a **leaf/petal** pointing right with a rounded counter (not a
circular one); the stem is a broad **wedge** tapering to a sharp point at the bottom-left; the o's
counter is large relative to its ring (~0.42 of the outer radius).

**Two treatments, and the reason for both:**
- `brand/onlypad-mark.svg` — **flat**, for the site (nav, footer, favicon). `#00AFF0` tile, pale-blue
  o `#C7E6F7`, white letter.
- `brand/onlypad-mark-neon.svg` — **neon**, matching their image's treatment: near-black `#05070A`
  ground, the whole mark in `#00AFF0`, a blurred copy underneath doubled for intensity and a **crisp
  copy on top so the counters stay sharp**. Blur alone (one pass, everything blurred) left the counters
  mushy — the crisp overlay is what fixes it.

**A glow fattens thin strokes, which is a trap.** The reference's stem looks chunky mostly because the
glow adds apparent width around it. Drawn flat, the same geometry reads as a spike. The flat version
therefore carries **thicker strokes than the neon one** on purpose — do not "correct" them to match.

Both exported at 1024 (`onlypad-mark-1024.png`, `onlypad-mark-neon-1024.png`). Verified in the nav at
30x30 and at 16px, favicon updated, 0 console errors, 46 maths assertions still green.

## OnlyPad — the board rebuilt as creator-funding cards

Twelfth brief: a reference of tilted neon-green creator cards, *"I want the live board to look like
that where they can launch and see their favorite creator."*

**The card grammar now matches the reference:** media panel → **circular** avatar overlapping its
bottom edge → identity inline beside it (name + tick, @handle) → a glowing green bar → a **crown pill**
paired with a **`$raised / $total`** figure in mono → a small meta line → the full-width action.

**The figures are the creator's Cut, and the bar is honest.** `earned = paid + escrow`, over
`generated` — that ratio *is* the launcher's split, so the bar differs coin to coin (50 / 60 / 70 / 80
across the board) and never has to be faked. **Whether the money has actually reached the creator is
carried by the pill colour and the locked tile, NOT by shrinking the bar to zero** — a 0% bar on the
four Fan-Launch coins would just look broken.

**The neon rule is relaxed on the board, deliberately and only there.** The ported CLAIM system says
"no gradients, no glows, no neon". The brand has since moved to a glowing neon mark and the user has
supplied two references in that language, so the board is allowed to glow: a green-tinted card border,
an outer shadow that deepens on hover, a glowing bar, and a radial wash behind the section. The rule
still holds everywhere else in the sheet. **Contrast still has to pass.**

**No stock photography.** The reference's media area holds a photo; ours is a flat panel with a
watermark. That is the same call as the monogram avatars — the slot is there for a creator's own image
when there is real content, and inventing suggestive imagery to fill it is not on.

### Two bugs this surfaced

1. **The ambient glow scrolled the whole page sideways.** `.boardglow` had
   `inset: -6% -14% 0 -14%`; the negative left/right pushed past the section and widened the document.
   `inset` must stay `0` — the falloff comes from the gradient, not the box.
2. **The action button floated mid-card.** Cards in a grid row stretch to equal height, so a card with
   no locked tile left its button stranded in the middle. `.ofaction` now takes `margin-top: auto`.

Also: `tick()` was still nudging `.ofstats b`, which the new card no longer has — it now recomputes
`.ofamount` and the bar width, and `o.generated` grows with every event so the ratio stays coherent.

Verified: 13 cards each with media, circular avatar, inline identity, bar, pill and amount; 4 amber
pills matching the 4 unclaimed coins; bar widths 50–80% varying by coin; glow and ambient present;
no horizontal overflow; 0 console errors; 46 maths assertions green.

Thirteenth brief: *"I want the concept of this website [claimpad.site] make sure to follow every details
